Marxist Chess (Meme)

Marxist Chess (Meme) is a chess variant where the only chess pieces are pawns. When they promote, they can move like a king, but is not affected by checks. Player wins by capturing all the enemy's pieces.

Inspired by the following meme:

Every Pawn has an equal share of power.
